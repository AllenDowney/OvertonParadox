# The Overton Paradox, Updated

Drafts of a series of *Probably Overthinking It* posts that update the analysis in Chapter 12 of *Probably Overthinking It* with General Social Survey data through 2024 and the age–period–cohort methods developed for *You Would Choose Now*.

- [Do People Get More Conservative With Age?](post1_age.md)
- [Technical Appendix](../notebooks/appendix.ipynb): the whole analysis as a notebook you can run on Colab.
- [The Overtron](https://allendowney.github.io/OvertonParadox/overtron/): run the same models in your browser with your own choice of questions.

The outline for the series is in `docs/blog_series.md`.
